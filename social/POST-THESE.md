# Post these

Eight images in this folder. Four messages, each as a square (feed) and a
vertical (story). Copy the caption, attach the image, post.

Everything below is written to stay inside Meta's financial advertising rules:
no promised return, no yield figure, no named property. Keep it that way if you
edit it.

---

## Where the leads actually come from

Instagram feed posts can't have clickable links. So one of these:

**Simplest — a Google Form.** Free, ten minutes, works today.
Go to forms.google.com, make a form with the questions below, hit Send → link →
copy. Put that link in your Instagram bio and directly in Facebook posts.
Answers land in a Google Sheet you own.

**Better once you're running ads — Meta Lead Ads.** The form opens inside
Facebook or Instagram, so nobody leaves the app and you need no website at all.
Set up in Ads Manager → Lead generation. Download leads as CSV, or connect them
to your email. Costs money per lead but converts better than sending people to
a link.

**Best long-term — your own site.** Everything already built. When you're ready,
`docs/GO-LIVE.md`.

Start with the Google Form. Move to Lead Ads when you're spending money.

### The questions to put in your form

Keep it to these. Every extra question costs you responses.

1. **What type of commercial property?** — Warehouse / Industrial / Storage /
   Small commercial / Open to options
2. **Approximate budget?** — Under $300k / $300–500k / $500–750k / $750k–$1M /
   $1M+ / Not sure yet
3. **Where are you looking?** — Melbourne / Greater Melbourne / Regional
   Victoria / Anywhere in Victoria / Open
4. **Are you finance-ready?** — Pre-approved / Assessment underway / Cash buyer /
   Not yet / Not sure
5. **How soon?** — Immediately / 1–3 months / 3–6 months / 6–12 months / Just
   researching
6. **Name**
7. **Email**
8. **Mobile**

Questions 4 and 5 are the ones that tell you who to ring first. Don't drop them.

Add this line at the bottom of the form:

> Information provided is general in nature and does not constitute financial,
> legal or investment advice. Registering does not create an obligation to
> purchase.

## Practical notes

**Posting rhythm.** Two a week is plenty. Rotate the four, then reuse — most of
your audience won't have seen the first round.

**Stories.** Use the `-story.png` versions with a link sticker. That's the only
way to get a tappable link on Instagram without paying.

**Replies.** Anyone who comments or DMs asking a question is a lead. Answer the
question, then ask what they're looking for. That conversation converts better
than anything on this page.

**What not to do.** Don't post a specific property, a price for one, a
development name, or anything about a return. Beyond the compliance problem, it
undoes the reason people register in the first place.

**If you start paying for reach:** Meta requires verification for financial
services advertising in Australia. Sort that before you build a campaign, not
after it gets rejected. Details in `docs/CAMPAIGNS.md`.
